Create an original fine-art still-life photograph that becomes the source artwork for a curated contemporary web colour palette titled "朱漆鎏金" (Vermilion Lacquer, Gilded). This is an imaginative homage to Chinese lacquer craft, not a reproduction or documentation of a specific historical object.

A quiet, cinematic horizontal composition. One object only: a rectangular vermilion lacquer box with a broad, matte gilded band running around the edge of its lid and small gilded fittings at the corners. The gilding is real aged gold: dull, warm, slightly uneven, worn thin at the corners so a darker bronze shows through, brightening only where the light grazes it. The lacquer is a deep, low-sheen vermilion with fine crackle and edges worn to brown. The box rests on a dark, matte wooden table at the lower left, occupying roughly a quarter of the frame. A tall, empty wall of warm grey lime plaster fills the rest of the picture, its surface calm and almost featureless. A single soft directional light enters from a high side window, so the gilt band becomes the one precise line of warmth while the box's shadow falls away into deep warm darkness. A slender, out-of-focus shadow of an unseen branch crosses the far wall, barely visible.

The palette is restrained: one dominant dark warm neutral, one deep vermilion, one substantive gilded gold, and a hushed plaster grey. Small areas of true darkness anchor the composition. Chinese negative space governs the frame: the wall is empty on purpose, the object sits off-centre, and the emptiness carries as much weight as the object. Materials are tactile and specific: micro-texture in the lacquer, a faint crackle along the edge, worn corners, and a matte metallic weight in the gilt that never becomes glitter.

Art direction: museum-quality contemporary Chinese visual sensibility; cinematic film still rather than advertising render. Shallow depth of field, real lens softness at the edges, gentle natural grain, quiet natural contrast, no heavy HDR, no neon saturation, no staged props. The composition should feel patient, spacious and expensive, with the discipline of a single well-made object in an empty room.

Technical: photorealistic macro-aware detail at the focal plane, soft falloff elsewhere, high dynamic range captured naturally, subtle film grain. Landscape aspect ratio 3:2, high quality, image fills the entire canvas edge to edge.
