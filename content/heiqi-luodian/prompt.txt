Create an original fine-art still-life photograph that becomes the source artwork for a curated contemporary web colour palette titled "黑漆螺钿" (Black Lacquer, Shell Inlay). This is an imaginative homage to Chinese lacquer craft, not a reproduction of a specific historical object.

A quiet, cinematic composition in a dark interior. One object only: a small black lacquer box inlaid with thin mother-of-pearl slivers along its lid, set low and slightly left of centre. The object fills a modest part of the frame and is surrounded by deep shadow rather than empty wall: the room's darkness, the falloff of a cool side light and the shallow focus provide the breathing space. The lacquer is near-black with a soft deep sheen; the inlay is quiet shell white that shows a faint cold iridescence only where the light touches it. A cool, very soft side light enters from the left, like moonlight through a paper window, leaving a faint plaster texture visible only near the light and letting the upper right settle into darkness.

The palette is restrained: near-black lacquer, shell white, cold silver-grey and one hushed blue-green reflection. Nothing shines like jewellery; the mother-of-pearl reads as bone and pearl, not glitter. Materials are tactile and specific: fine crackle in the black lacquer, hairline inlay seams, slightly worn corners, a dust-free matte surface. The composition holds the object with room to breathe, without turning the frame into a mostly empty rectangle.
Art direction: museum-quality contemporary Chinese visual sensibility; cinematic film still rather than advertising render. Shallow depth of field, real lens softness away from the focal plane, gentle natural grain, quiet natural contrast, no heavy HDR, no neon saturation, no staged props. Let the amount of breathing space follow the motif: a small object needs air around it, a material study may fill more of the frame and rest on shadow instead. Do not reserve a fixed half or third as empty background, and do not centre the subject symmetrically.

Technical: photorealistic macro-aware detail at the focal plane, high dynamic range captured naturally, subtle film grain. Landscape aspect ratio 3:2, high quality, image fills the entire canvas edge to edge.
