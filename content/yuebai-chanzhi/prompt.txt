Create an original fine-art photograph that becomes the source artwork for a curated contemporary web colour palette titled "月白缠枝" (Moon-White Scrollwork). This is an imaginative homage to Chinese textile ornament, not a reproduction of a specific historical textile.

A quiet, high-key cinematic composition, deliberately light and airy. One subject only: a length of moon-white silk, faintly cool, with an extremely fine continuous scrollwork vine worked in pale silver-grey thread across its surface. The cloth is loosely draped and occupies the centre of the frame, falling in soft low folds; because the material itself is the subject, the picture stays close to it, and the breathing space comes from the silk dissolving into very soft light at the edges rather than from an empty half-frame. Diffused morning light from above and to the left, almost shadowless, with the faintest cool shading in the folds.

The palette is restrained and quiet: moon white, cool pale grey, a whisper of sky blue in the shadows and a fine pale silver line. Contrast stays low but the surface must remain legible; no blown highlights. Materials are tactile and specific: visible silk fibre, the slight relief of the embroidered vine, soft creases, no printed pattern, no embroidery glitter, no lace.
Art direction: museum-quality contemporary Chinese visual sensibility; cinematic film still rather than advertising render. Shallow depth of field, real lens softness away from the focal plane, gentle natural grain, quiet natural contrast, no heavy HDR, no neon saturation, no staged props. Let the amount of breathing space follow the motif: a small object needs air around it, a material study may fill more of the frame and rest on shadow instead. Do not reserve a fixed half or third as empty background, and do not centre the subject symmetrically.

Technical: photorealistic macro-aware detail at the focal plane, high dynamic range captured naturally, subtle film grain. Landscape aspect ratio 3:2, high quality, image fills the entire canvas edge to edge.
